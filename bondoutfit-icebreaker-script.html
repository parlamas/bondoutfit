Bondoutfit

Icebreaker script — walking into a store · English / Dansk
Opening line
🇬🇧"Hi, I noticed you don't have online booking for fittings — is that something you've thought about?"
🇩🇰"Hej, jeg lagde mærke til, at I ikke har online tidsbestilling til prøveafprøvning — er det noget, I har overvejet?"
If they seem interested — go straight into a quick live demo on your phone
🇬🇧"I run a platform called Bondoutfit — customers book a fitting time with your store online, and get a discount when they show up and buy. Can I show you on my phone? Takes about a minute."
🇩🇰"Jeg står bag en platform, der hedder Bondoutfit — kunder booker en tid hos jer online og får en rabat, når de dukker op og køber noget. Må jeg lige vise dig det på min telefon? Det tager kun et minut."
If they're polite but busy — hand the leaflet, offer to return
🇬🇧"No rush — everything's on this leaflet, and I'm happy to swing by another time if you'd like to see it properly."
🇩🇰"Ingen stress — alt står på denne folder, og jeg kommer meget gerne forbi en anden gang, hvis du vil se det ordentligt."
If they seem uninterested — leave the door open, don't push
🇬🇧"No worries at all — the details are all in there if you're ever curious."
🇩🇰"Helt i orden — detaljerne står derinde, hvis du nogensinde bliver nysgerrig."
