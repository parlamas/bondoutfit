Bondoutfit
Turn window-shoppers into booked visits.
With Bondoutfit, your customers can book a time at your store online — and get a discount when they show up and make a purchase. You get fewer wasted visits, more traffic on slow days, and one simple dashboard to manage it all.

How it works

1
The customer finds your store

2

3

You know who's coming and when, so you can prepare.
4
They visit and get rewarded
On arrival, they unlock a discount they can use on their purchase.
